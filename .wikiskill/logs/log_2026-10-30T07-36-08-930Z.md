# Harness² & WikiSkill Experience Log: No Approval or Verified Savings Without Geometric & Quantitative Evidence

- **Dimension**: `DOM` (Domain / Practitioner Knowledge)
- **Category**: `Epistemic-Zero-Trust-Audit`
- **Confidence**: `high`

## Cue (Shape of Situation)
When building or testing architectural validation, 3D BIM consistency checks, or Value Engineering (VE) cost-saving engines (validation_engine.py, model_3d_change_detector.py, design_optimization_engine.py)

## Evidence (Trajectory Failure & Resolution)
- **Observed Failure / Divergence**: Empty or partial inputs triggered 5 hardcoded PASS checks in run_3d_bim_planning_validation(), returned preset 196,200 ILS savings on empty data_summary for cataloged projects, enforced min floors max(18.0, ...) and max(25.0, ...) sqm savings with fabricated stall relocation narratives for custom uploads, and converted missing per-facade dry cladding data to 0.0 when only one facade had material fields.
- **Applied Fix**: Separate Finding, Proposal, and Verification; return UNKNOWN/INSUFFICIENT_DATA instead of PASS or fabricated savings when geometry/quantities are missing; remove minimum savings floors (max(18.0,...), max(25.0,...)); clamp facade conversions per-facade against verified dry-cladding area; and test negative/abstention cases (E01-E08) where the system MUST NOT emit recommendations or approvals.

## Transferable Lesson
Never emit PASS or verified cost savings without measured geometric and quantitative evidence: (1) missing inputs must yield UNKNOWN/INSUFFICIENT_DATA, never default PASS or synthetic rooms; (2) never apply positive minimum floors (max(18, ...), max(25, ...)) to VE savings or fabricate stall-relocation proofs without extracted geometry; (3) evaluate material availability per-facade (never flip all facades to exact mode via any() and default missing facades to 0); (4) enforce abstention EVALS (E01-E08) verifying that empty/partial models produce 0 approvals and 0 verified savings.
