# Harness² & WikiSkill Experience Log: Separate System Sync Metadata from Architectural Planning Findings
- **Date**: 2026-10-28T07:05:52.724Z
- **Dimension**: `DOM` (Domain / Practitioner Knowledge)
- **Category**: `UI`
- **Confidence**: `high`

## Cue (Shape of Situation)
When tracking 3D model updates, SHA-256 fingerprint changes, or automated re-audit status in an architectural quality audit pipeline

## Evidence (Trajectory Failure & Resolution)
- **Observed Failure / Divergence**: Injected a synthetic -3D-SYNC card ('בוצעה בדיקה תכנונית מלאה חדשה בעקבות זיהוי עדכון במודל ה-3D הבסיסי') into payload['notes'], polluting the architectural planning findings list and pin count with system status metadata
- **Applied Fix**: Removed sync_note injection from model_3d_change_detector.py, kept sync metadata strictly inside payload['model_3d_sync_status'] for header/HUD badges, and added defensive filters in webapp/app.js and webapp/audit_3d_overlay.js

## Transferable Lesson
Never inject system operational events (such as 3D model fingerprint sync or re-audit triggers) as items in payload['notes']; store sync state exclusively in payload['model_3d_sync_status'] and reserve payload['notes'] strictly for actual architectural, statutory, and VE planning findings
