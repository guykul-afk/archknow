## Cue (Shape of Situation)
When analyzing an L-shaped bedroom with a 50-100 cm alcove/step-out adjacent to a bathroom (such as FB3-W-04, 310x310/360 cm with a 50x140 cm alcove south of the 230x170 cm bathroom) and recommending wardrobe placement

## Evidence (Trajectory Failure & Resolution)
- **Observed Failure / Divergence**: FB3-W-04 and FB3-OPT-APT1 recommended placing a wardrobe inside the 50x140 cm alcove (difference between 360 and 310 cm south of the bathroom wall), without checking that the bedroom entrance door is installed in that exact 50x140 cm vestibule and swings into it, so placing a closet there physically blocks the room entrance.
- **Applied Fix**: Added TEST-11-DOORWAY-VESTIBULE-CLOSET-GUARD to design_optimization_engine.py and updated FB3-W-04, FB3-OPT-APT1, FB3-E-04, FB3-OPT-APT2, H44-PH-03, project_summary_builder.py, spatial_3d_mapper.py, and webapp/audit_3d_overlay.js to keep doorway entry vestibules 100% clear and place wardrobes along interior walls east/outside of the door swing.

## Transferable Lesson
Before recommending a wardrobe inside a room alcove/recess (e.g. 360 vs 310 cm step-out behind a bathroom), always inspect the 2D CAD floor plan to verify where the room entrance door is located. If the entrance door is on the alcove wall or swings through it, classify the alcove strictly as a Doorway Entry Vestibule (מבואת דלת כניסה) that must remain 100% unobstructed, and place the wardrobe along a clear interior wall outside the door swing.
