## Cue (Shape of Situation)
When rendering architectural audit pins/boxes in both 2D floor plans and 3D Three.js/WebGL Dollhouse views, or when building 1:50 top-down orthographic camera presets

## Evidence (Trajectory Failure & Resolution)
- **Observed Failure / Divergence**: In feierberg3, Apt 2 (#28, 85.5m2) was on the Left in 2D but on the Right in 3D, and orthoQuickBtn used theta=-Math.PI/2. Fixed by mirroring feierberg3 upper floors X->-X, calibrating SHEET_2D_TO_3D_TRANSFORMS across all 32 sheets (178/178 notes locked in spatial_lock_registry.json), and locking floor-sliced camera theta to +1.57.
- **Applied Fix**: Always verify 3 invariants between 2D sheets and 3D models: (1) Base 3D geometry wing parity (Left/West X<0 vs Right/East X>0 must match the 2D Gramoshka sheet 1:1 without X-axis mirroring), (2) Coordinate tuple ordering (bbox_3d is [x0, z0, x1, z1, elev0, elev1] whereas pin_3d and camera.target are [x, elev, z]), and (3) Camera azimuth theta for floor-sliced/ortho views must stay in [+1.25, +1.85] (+Math.PI/2, South looking North) because negative theta (-Math.PI/2, North looking South) flips Left and Right wings.
