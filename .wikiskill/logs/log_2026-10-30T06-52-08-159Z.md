# Harness² & WikiSkill Experience Log: Guard 3D Model Sync in model_3d_change_detector.py Against Redirect Stubs
- **Date**: 2026-10-30T06:52:08.159Z
- **Dimension**: `INV` (Invariant / Agent Machinery)
- **Category**: `Build & Static Hosting`
- **Confidence**: `high`

## Cue (Shape of Situation)
When syncing or re-auditing 3D models across CENTRAL_MODELS_ROOT, exec_folder, and webapp/models/<pid>/ via model_3d_change_detector.py or export_static_data.py

## Evidence (Trajectory Failure & Resolution)
- **Observed Failure / Divergence**: consolidate_newer_source_files_for_project and sync_single_project_3d_model in model_3d_change_detector.py lacked a ZERO-COPY-CENTRAL-MODEL-REDIRECT / <5KB size guard, copying 2KB redirect stubs from exec_folder into CENTRAL_MODELS_ROOT and webapp/models/<pid>/, causing Firebase Hosting iframes to redirect to 404 Page Not Found
- **Applied Fix**: Added ZERO-COPY-CENTRAL-MODEL-REDIRECT and <5KB size guards to consolidate_newer_source_files_for_project and sync_single_project_3d_model, added reverse-healing from webapp/models/<pid>/ back to CENTRAL_MODELS_ROOT, and passed rebuild_artifacts=False during export_static_data.py loop to avoid duplicate concurrent file writes

## Transferable Lesson
Enforce ZERO-COPY-CENTRAL-MODEL-REDIRECT and <5KB size guards in ALL sync paths (both export_static_data.py and model_3d_change_detector.py), reverse-heal CENTRAL_MODELS_ROOT from intact webapp/models/<pid>/ viewers if a stub is encountered, and verify every webapp/models/<pid>/<viewer>.html is >1MB before deploying to Firebase Hosting.
