## Cue (Shape of Situation)
When proposing a recessed zero-line closet niche (Z-profile / קו-אפס) in a bedroom partition wall after applying TEST-8 corridor protection

## Evidence (Trajectory Failure & Resolution)
- **Observed Failure / Divergence**: In Feierberg 3 Note #3 (FB3-W-03, Northern Room 280x370 cm in Apt 1), the engine automatically recommended a recessed Z-profile closet niche in the internal longitudinal wall without checking that the East wall borders the General Bathroom (230x170 cm with bathtub) and the West wall borders the 20 cm concrete stairwell (350 cm), making a closet niche impossible without shrinking the bathroom.
- **Applied Fix**: Added TEST-10-CLOSET-NICHE-BATHROOM-GUARD to design_optimization_engine.py and updated FB3-W-03, FB3-OPT-APT1, project_summary_builder.py, spatial_3d_mapper.py, and webapp/audit_3d_overlay.js to reject recessed closet niches on walls bordering bathrooms, concrete shafts, or Mamad walls.

## Transferable Lesson
Before recommending a recessed closet niche (Z-profile / קו-אפס), inspect all 4 room walls on the 2D CAD plan: never recommend a closet niche on a wall bordering a bathroom (which would shrink the bathroom and eliminate the bathtub), a concrete stair/elevator/Mamad wall, or a kitchen cabinet wall; document the lack of closet niche as a rigid geometric constraint.
