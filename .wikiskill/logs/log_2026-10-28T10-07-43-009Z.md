## Cue (Shape of Situation)
When proposing basement shoring contraction that affects an end parking stall or relocating pump/water-tank rooms under a ramp

## Evidence (Trajectory Failure & Resolution)
- **Observed Failure / Divergence**: design_optimization_engine.py (H44-OPT-UG1, TEST-3, TEST-5, TEST-6), project_analyzer_service.py (H44-BSM-01), webapp/audit_3d_overlay.js (basement_ve_full)
- **Applied Fix**: 1. Never place under-ramp utility rooms outside the exact upper ramp footprint (where H_clear >= 2.15m); verify on both the Basement and Ground Floor plans where the ramp slope actually starts so adjacent stalls (such as Stall #17 in Hafetz Chaim 44, located under the open ground courtyard west of ramp line +45.52) are never overwritten. 2. Never relocate a cancelled stall into an active ramp/maneuvering aisle (5.20-6.00m) where it either narrows the aisle below statutory width or pushes adjacent stalls outside the shoring piles (increasing contour). 3. Always audit all parking levels including the Ground Floor surface lot (noting that uncovered surface parking adds 0 sqm to built area, while checking preserved trees and statutory percolation zones). If no valid internal or ground-floor bay exists without expanding shoring or blocking an aisle, cancel the shoring pocket cut and reclassify it as protected_ramp_and_maneuvering_deducted_sqm (0 ILS).
