## Cue (Shape of Situation)
When mapping 2D plan annotations and 3D spatial pins/volumes (spatial_3d) for multi-floor residential models (such as Feierberg 3 or Yatkovski 7)

## Evidence (Trajectory Failure & Resolution)
- **Observed Failure / Divergence**: User reported Note #18 (FB3-OPT-APT1) spatial marker mismatch in Feierberg 3; fixed in spatial_3d_mapper.py, design_optimization_engine.py, project_analyzer_service.py, and webapp/audit_3d_overlay.js.
- **Applied Fix**: Never rely on generic percentage-to-meter linear scaling (_infer_generic_spatial_3d) because 2D architectural sheets have asymmetric title-block margins and may orient wings opposite to the 3D Three.js X-axis (e.g., Feierberg 3 Apt 1 is on the right in 2D x_pct>42% but West X<0 in 3D). Always extract exact 1:1 room coordinates from the 3D model's FLOORS/extracted_plans.json, map multi-level floor IDs explicitly (b1, b2, kar, f4, f9, f10, f6), and verify 2D percentage boxes by rendering a visual overlay grid onto the actual sheet PNG.
