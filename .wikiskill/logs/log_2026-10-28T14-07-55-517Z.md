## Cue (Shape of Situation)
When proposing bedroom enlargement / corridor shortening or underground parking stall relocation across residential floorplans and basements

## Evidence (Trajectory Failure & Resolution)
- **Observed Failure / Divergence**: User correction on Hafetz Chaim 44 (H44-OPT-APT2, H44-E-03/04, H44-OPT-UG1, H44-BSM-01) and Feierberg 3 (FB3-W-03/05, FB3-OPT-APT1)
- **Applied Fix**: TEST-8 (Corridor Bathroom Door Clearance): Never enlarge a bedroom at the expense of the corridor or shorten/narrow the corridor in front of the General Bathroom (אמבטיה כללית) entrance door, as doing so blocks the bathroom doorway; use internal room niches instead. TEST-9 (Zero Parking Contour Expansion): Never relocate a parking stall if its new position enlarges the overall basement excavation/shoring contour or fails to yield a major planning advantage; in tightly packed basements (e.g., Hafetz Chaim 44), preserve 100% of stalls in their original CAD coordinates (0 stalls moved) and place technical rooms strictly under the upper ramp.
