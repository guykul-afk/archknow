# Harness² & WikiSkill Experience Log: UI-Layout-Grid
- **Date**: 2026-11-08T13:55:39.955Z
- **Dimension**: `DOM` (Domain / Practitioner Knowledge)
- **Category**: `UI-Layout-Grid`
- **Confidence**: `high`

## Cue (Shape of Situation)
When collapsing a panel in a CSS Grid layout (e.g., .split-workspace.doc-collapsed) or placing floating restore buttons

## Evidence (Trajectory Failure & Resolution)
- **Observed Failure / Divergence**: Fixed blank white screen bug in webapp/styles.css (.split-workspace.doc-collapsed) and webapp/index.html (#plan-toolbar-restore-bar, #edge-restore-doc-btn)
- **Applied Fix**: Never combine display: none on the first grid child with grid-template-columns: 0px minmax(0, 1fr), as removing the first child from grid flow shifts the second child (#visual-panel) into the 0px column and blanks the entire viewport; instead change grid-template-columns to minmax(0, 1fr) and keep floating restore bars as direct fixed children of <body> outside collapsible containers.

## Transferable Lesson
