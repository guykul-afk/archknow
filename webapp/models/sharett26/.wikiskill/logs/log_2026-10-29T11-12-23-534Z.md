## Cue (Shape of Situation)
When extracting 3D walls, roof levels, and facades from an Israeli Garmushka DWFX/XPS drawing (FixedPage.fpage)

## Evidence (Trajectory Failure & Resolution)
- **Observed Failure / Divergence**: Verified against high-res PyMuPDF renders of 6.5.26-hagasha.dwfx (plan_upper_roof.png, plan_floor_9_roof.png, plan_floor_8.png, ground_and_south_elev_real.png) and runtime test_runtime.js (14 floors, 11824 meshes, 0 errors).
- **Applied Fix**: 1) Never filter XPS <Path> elements solely by the first M(x_init,y_init) coordinate because XPS groups strokes across multiple plans into a single <Path>; filter per subpath sp[0]. 2) Never polygonize StrokeThickness='12' on roof plans (floor_9_roof/upper_roof), as th='12' represents solar collector grids and slope arrows (which turn into spurious vertical pillars); extract only the core stair/elevator penthouses (+31.32->+33.77/+34.27) and build the +32.37m solid perimeter parapet and integrated concrete pergolas from the exact 20-vertex roof footprint. 3) Enforce water-tight exterior wall envelopes with exact CAD opening cutouts (vitrines, windows, and full-height dark aluminum laundry louver screens) and clip 2D CAD lines strictly to the building footprint.
